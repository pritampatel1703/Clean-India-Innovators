🗑️

What is Waste?

Waste is any substance which is discarded after primary use, or is worthless, defective and of no use.

🍌

Types of Waste

Mainly classified into: Wet (Organic), Dry (Recyclable), Biomedical, Hazardous, and E-waste.

🚮

Waste Segregation

Separating waste into Dry (Blue Bin) and Wet (Green Bin) is the first and most crucial step for effective recycling.

♻️

The 5 R's

Refuse what you don't need, Reduce what you use, Reuse items, Repurpose old things, and Recycle.

✂️

Best out of Waste

Get creative! Turn waste materials like bottles, cans, and paper into useful crafts, decor, or utility items.

🛑

Refusing Plastic

Single-use plastics are a major pollutant. Say NO to plastic bags, straws, and bottles to stop pollution at the source.

🌸

Innovative Recycling

Innovative solutions include making new products from recycled plastics and turning waste temple flowers into incense sticks.

🍂

Composition

Composting is the natural process of recycling organic matter, such as leaves and food scraps, into a valuable fertilizer.
