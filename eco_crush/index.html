How to Play

Match 3 items to sort the waste!

  • 👆 Select: Click/Tap an item.
  • ↔️ Swap: Click/Tap an adjacent item.
  • ✅ Match: Align 3+ same items.
  • 🧹 Swipe: You can also swipe to swap!
